This release changes how an air-conditioned room behind a Navizone is
controlled, and makes the absence something you set in one go.

### ⚠️ Navizone rooms: the house's mode moves to its own select

A room's climate entity used to mix two things in one menu : Heat, Cool,
Auto and Dehumidify changed the whole house, Off stopped only that room.
Each room now has a **System service** select, the app's own dropdown :
System off, Heat, Cool, Auto, Dehumidify. System off is the app's general
stop, which Home Assistant could not send before. The room's climate keeps
off and the mode the house is running. An automation that asks a room for
heat will now fail while the house cools : change the select instead.

### 🔧 Fixes you will notice

A change now shows in every room at once, rather than room by room as
polls come round. Editing an away date while the absence was off could
leave the house away and the device not ; a date now only goes out with
the switch.

### 🌡️ Absence and consumption

`cozytouch.set_away_mode` turns the absence on with its dates in one
action, `cozytouch.clear_away_mode` ends it, and Assist can use both.
While it runs, your climate entities show the `away` preset. Devices that
report consumption get today's energy, its cost and water. That was built
from another user's water heater and has not been seen on a Navizone,
which reports none.

### 🏷️ A tidier room page

On a Navizone room, the override and air circulation timers now sit
together in the main section, and whether an override is running is shown
and on by default. The per-room absence and the heating status, which only
echoed the hub or never moved, go to Diagnostic ; the heating status is
off by default on new installs. These were chosen from the Navizone's
room page, and any other device reporting the same readings moves with
them.
