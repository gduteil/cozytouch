The biggest update since the integration exists. You no longer add your
devices one by one: sign in once, and every device on your Cozytouch account
comes with it, whatever its model.

### ⬆️ Coming from 1.4

Nothing to do. Restart Home Assistant after the update: your devices regroup
under your account on their own, and your entities, automations, dashboards
and history stay as they are.

### ✨ What's new

- 👤 **One account, all your devices.** Sign in once; add or remove devices
  later from the integration page.
- 🔎 **Every model recognised.** A device nobody had reported arrives with
  its real name and its own modes, no more `Unknown product`.
- 🗓️ **Your weekly program as a calendar.** Edit it from the schedule card,
  from an automation (`cozytouch.set_schedule`), or by voice.
- ✈️ **Away mode in one action.** `cozytouch.set_away_mode` turns the
  absence on with its dates, `cozytouch.clear_away_mode` ends it, and your
  climates show the `away` preset meanwhile.
- 🗣️ **Assist.** Ask it to read the program, hold a temperature until a
  given time, or set your absence ("on part du 3 au 10 octobre").
- 🚨 **Faults in Repairs.** A device fault shows up with Atlantic's own
  explanation and repair steps, and clears itself when it is fixed.
- ⚡ **Today's consumption.** Energy, peak and off-peak, cost and water,
  on installations that report it.
- 🔥 **Much more per device.** Burner and pumps on boilers, compressor and
  backup heater on heat pumps, tank temperatures and remaining hot water on
  water heaters.
- 🌀 **Air conditioners.** Air circulation is a fan entity, and Navizone
  rooms get a select for the whole house's mode, general stop included.
- 🎯 **Automation triggers** for when the program changes what it asks for.
- 🌍 **Spanish, German and Italian**, on top of English and French.
- ⏱️ **Faster.** A change shows right away instead of at the next poll, and
  the poll interval is yours to set (15 s to 10 min).

### 🔧 Fixes

- Updates no longer stop until the next restart.
- A command the cloud refuses now tells you so.
- A switch no longer flips back right after you toggle it.

### 👋 Retired

The day-by-day program sensors (switched off, the calendar replaces them),
the eco switch on air conditioners (they have no eco mode) and the min/max
temperature readings.
