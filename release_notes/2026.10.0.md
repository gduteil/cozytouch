This release rebuilds the integration around your Cozytouch account: one
entry for the account with every device under it, and a device recognised
from what it reports rather than from a list somebody had to extend.

### ⚠️ Coming from 1.4: delete your entries and add your account again

1.4 made one entry per device, and this release cannot load them : after
updating, each shows a migration error, and Repairs says what to do. Delete
**every** Cozytouch entry, add the integration again with your login, and
all your devices come at once. Entity ids change, so update the automations
and dashboards that use them. The per-day program sensors, the eco switch
on air conditioners and the min/max temperature readings are gone for good.

### 🏷️ One account, and every model

Devices are added when you sign in, or later from the integration page.
Each is typed from the product id it reports, the way Atlantic's app does,
so a model nobody has reported still arrives named, with its own modes,
instead of `Unknown product`. Over 600 model ids are named from Atlantic's
catalogue. What a device reports that nothing names yet raises a notice
asking for its diagnostics file.

### 🌡️ More of each device

Boilers show their burner, flame and pumps, heat pumps their compressor and
backup heater, water heaters their tank temperatures and remaining hot water.
Air circulation is a fan. A Navizone room has a select for the house's mode,
general stop included. Faults appear in Repairs in Atlantic's words, the
absence is set in one action, and devices that report it show today's
consumption.

### 📋 The week as a calendar

Each weekly program is a calendar you can edit from its card, from the
`set_schedule` action, or by voice.

### 🔧 Fixes

Polling no longer freezes until a restart, a refused command is reported
instead of silently dropped, and a switch stops bouncing back after a
toggle. Spanish, German and Italian join English and French.
