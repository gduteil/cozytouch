Most of this release is your devices finally telling Home Assistant what
they are.

### 🔧 Fixes you will notice

A value you set from Home Assistant used to get overwritten a second later
by the refresh that followed it, so a setpoint would snap back on its own.
It holds now, on every device. Room 2's comfort setpoint was labelled as
something else entirely. And the override duration is offered as the hours
the Cozytouch app offers, rather than any number you like — the device only
ever accepted those.

### 📋 Atlantic Hybrellia

Its weekly schedule, its tank setpoints and its overrides were being written
somewhere the device does not read. All three work now. If you have a
Hybrellia and gave up on its schedule, try it again.

### 🏷️ Devices and sensors with real names

631 model ids that showed up as `Unknown product (…)` now carry the name
Atlantic gives them. Alongside that, we stopped guessing what a capability
means and started reading Atlantic's own description of it — the label, the
unit, and the words behind a value that used to be a bare number. Boilers
gain their burner, flame and pumps ; heat pumps their compressor, inputs and
backup heater ; water heaters their smart grid and what is actually heating
them.

### 🔍 If your device still reads as unknown

That is now rare enough to be worth reporting. The diagnostics dump carries
Atlantic's description of every capability your device reports, which is
usually enough to add it without a single question back.
